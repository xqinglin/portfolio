BEHIND THE WORK

About me

Hi, I’m Xiaoqing Lin
You can call me Lynn

I’m a machine learning engineer based in Seattle, with experience across Netflix, Coupang, and Amazon. My background spans machine learning, advertising systems, and backend engineering. I enjoy understanding how systems fit together, working through problems with teammates, and becoming someone people can count on.

The resources behind the technology

I grew up in Shanxi, a coal-producing province in China. That connection to home shapes how I think about computing. The technology may feel invisible, but the energy and resources behind it are real.

I care about shutting down idle machines, choosing the compute a task actually needs, and making ML pipelines and services more efficient. Saving resources gives everyday engineering decisions a deeper purpose. Even a small improvement feels meaningful when it helps us waste less.

A different way to create

Outside work, I make AI video ads and short stories. I began experimenting with image generation out of curiosity. With these new tools, what could I create?

My first music video grew from both excitement about AI and concern for people whose working lives were changing around them. It ended with a middle-aged man meeting his younger self. Rather than receiving advice from the future, the younger self offered encouragement, passing his courage and determination forward.

Watch《革自己的命》on YouTube ↗

I’m drawn to that combination of technical possibility and human feeling. I enjoy exploring what the tools can do, but what matters most to me is whether the result expresses something I care about.

Beyond the next goal

When something feels meaningful, I tend to give it my full attention. I find joy in bringing an idea to life and in seeing it resonate with someone else. I’m also learning to leave room for health, rest, and everyday life, without needing every moment to lead to another achievement.

Whether I’m building a system or telling a story, I want to make something useful, thoughtful, or simply worth sharing.
